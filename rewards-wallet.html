Wallet entries

Every change to your balance is a ledger entry with its own ID, status and reason.

TypeMeaningStatus
Reward creditA reward is added to your wallet.Planned
RedemptionYou spend or redeem part of your balance.Planned
ReversalA credited reward is cancelled, linked to the original entry, with a reason.Planned
AdjustmentA correction to an amount, with a reason.Planned
ExchangeConverting one unit into another, if offered.Planned

Reward states

  • Pending

    Activity done; waiting for confirmation.

  • Credited

    Reward is in your wallet.

  • Reversed

    Reward removed; the reason is shown.

  • Corrected

    Amount changed; both amounts are shown.

Withdrawals

Follow every request to the end

Methods, limits and eligibility will be published before launch. This is the planned lifecycle.

  1. 1

    Requested

    You submit a withdrawal request.
  2. 2

    Under review

    The request is being processed.
  3. 3

    Completed

    The payout is confirmed.
  4. 4

    Rejected or reversed

    The request is refused, or a completed payout is reversed. A reason category is shown.
