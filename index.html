FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Is VELOOP Rewards live?

Not yet. It is under development, so features on this page describe what is planned and may change.

How much can I earn?

We do not publish earning figures yet. Amounts will be shown clearly inside the app once rewards are live.

What are VEs, XP, Gems and Tokens?

They are the platform's reward and progress units. Exact definitions and conversion rules will be announced before launch.

How will withdrawals work?

Requests move through requested, review and a final result. Available methods and limits will be published before launch.

What data do you collect?

Only what is needed to run accounts and understand how the product is used. Details will be in the privacy policy.

Is the waitlist form saving my email?

Not in this demo page. The form only shows how signup will feel.
