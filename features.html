Fair by design

Clear rules, visible status

  • Rewards count as earned only once they are credited.
  • Pending, credited and reversed rewards are labelled, with a reason where one applies.
  • External partners (ads, surveys, offers) can delay or reverse results. VELOOP shows that instead of hiding it.
  • A sign-up is never treated as a successful referral by itself.
  • No earning amounts are promised on this website. Amounts appear in the app once it is live.
