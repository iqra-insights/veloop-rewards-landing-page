Help center

Search answers about accounts, earning, wallet, withdrawals, referrals and privacy.

How do I create an account?

Accounts open at launch. Join the waitlist on the home page to be notified.

What details do you collect?

Only what is needed to run accounts and understand product use. Full details will be in the privacy policy.

How much can I earn?

Earning amounts are not published yet. They will be shown inside the app once rewards are live.

Why is my reward pending?

Some rewards wait for confirmation from the task or an external partner. The status changes to credited or reversed.

What is a streak?

A run of consecutive days on which you claimed your daily reward.

What do VEs, XP, Gems and Tokens mean?

They are the platform units. Final definitions and conversion rules will be announced before launch.

Why was a reward reversed?

A credited reward can be cancelled, for example if a partner later rejects the completion. The reason is shown.

How do withdrawals work?

A request moves through requested, under review and a final result: completed or rejected, with a reason.

Which withdrawal methods exist?

Methods and limits will be published before launch.

When does a referral count?

When the invited person registers and then becomes active under the referral rules. Sign-up alone is not enough.

Does this website store my email?

No. The waitlist form on this demo website does not save anything.

Does this website use cookies?

No cookies and no browser storage are used.
